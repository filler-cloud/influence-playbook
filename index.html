Project Intent

This Influence Playbook is a real tool to help me apply what I learned about influence in every part of my life — from my work at BCG, to my long-term goal of building a sports investment group, to rebuilding my personal resilience after ACL recovery. I built this Playbook to be practical, personal, and forward-looking.

The lessons from class aren't just theory — they are tactics I can use every day: building coalitions before meetings, framing choices strategically, leading with warmth, anchoring change with early wins, and showing empathy as a source of strength. Influence starts small and builds quietly, and this Playbook is how I plan to keep moving forward intentionally.

Biggest Takeaways from Influence Class

🌍 See Through Their Eyes First:

Understand what your audience truly values before you present your case — it changes the entire influence dynamic.

Historic Reference: Pep Guardiola succeeded at Barcelona by seeing what each star player needed to thrive emotionally, not just tactically.

⚡ Momentum Beats Perfection:

Stacking small wins builds unstoppable belief — you don’t need to have the \"perfect\" project or pitch to move forward.

Historic Reference: Leicester City won the Premier League through consistent small victories, not by dominating every match.

🤝 Coalitions Win Championships:

Influence isn't about being the loudest in the room — it's about building alliances quietly before big moments.

Historic Reference: Michael Jordan’s success with the Bulls was built on trust with Scottie Pippen, Phil Jackson, and leadership, not solo performances.

💬 Lead with Empathy, Win with Strength:

Real authority comes from showing you understand others' needs before asserting your own agenda.

Historic Reference: Tony Dungy transformed the Colts with a culture of calm belief and empathy, not fear and control.

🏃‍♂️ Every Day is Influence Practice:

Influence isn’t reserved for \"big moments\" — every conversation is an opportunity to build trust and credibility.

Historic Reference: Serena Williams’ greatness wasn’t built in Grand Slam finals — it was crafted daily, behind the scenes, through influence and discipline.

🏆 Make Others the Heroes:

True influence uplifts others — creating shared victories instead of solo spotlights.

Historic Reference: Steve Kerr’s Warriors dynasty celebrated team-first culture, empowering Steph Curry's unselfish leadership style.
