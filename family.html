Family

Family is the first — and most important — place where influence is built. Here, influence isn’t about persuasion or strategy; it’s about showing up, listening fully, and building a foundation of trust over years. Love is the ultimate long game of influence.

Influence Moves to Use:

  • Lead with Presence, Not Advice: Most of the time, being there without fixing anything is the strongest move you can make with family.
  • Recognize and Celebrate Quiet Wins: Celebrate the things that aren't traditionally praised (small progress, effort, quiet resilience).
  • Ask Instead of Assuming: Ask open-ended questions about dreams, challenges, and support — don’t assume you know.
  • Give Belief as a Gift: Be the consistent voice that believes in them, even when they doubt themselves.
  • Anchor Actions to Love, Not Outcomes: Every act of influence (support, advice, encouragement) should feel unconditional — tied to love, not achievement.

Mini Challenge:

Call or text one family member this week and ask them a question about something they’re excited about — not just \"how's work\" or \"how’s school.\" Listen fully.

Questions to Ask Myself:

  • Am I showing up without conditions or expectations?
  • Have I offered belief and encouragement proactively?
  • Am I leading with love, not results?

carry with you the lessons you've learned.
