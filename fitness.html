Fitness

Fitness isn't just physical — it's mental, emotional, and identity-building. After my ACL tear, recovery taught me that influence begins inside: by shaping how I frame challenges, celebrate micro-wins, and persist on days when nobody else is watching.

Influence Moves to Use:

  • Frame Recovery as Growth: Instead of \"getting back\" to where I was, frame this journey as building a stronger, more resilient version of myself.
  • Anchor Goals to Small Visible Wins: Set micro-milestones (e.g., regaining a range of motion, hitting a rehab benchmark) and celebrate every one.
  • Leverage Public Commitment: Share my training milestones publicly with trusted people to create accountability through social commitment.
  • Reframe Setbacks as Setups: Bad weeks aren't failures; they are setups for comebacks. Frame every setback as a chance to refocus smarter.
  • Visualize Future Victories: Regularly spend time visualizing myself sprinting, cutting, lifting again — making the future emotionally real.

Mini Challenge:

Set one small, measurable fitness milestone for the next 7 days — and text it to someone you trust for built-in accountability.

Questions to Ask Myself:

  • Am I focusing on today's controllable wins instead of fixating on how far I still have to go?
  • Have I reframed today's challenges into growth opportunities?
  • Am I celebrating my progress consistently, even small wins?

carry with you the lessons you've learned.
