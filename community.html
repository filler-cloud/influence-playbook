Community

Influence inside a community isn’t about winning arguments or commanding attention — it’s about building trust, sharing energy, and creating spaces where others feel seen and valued. It's slow, steady work: the kind that builds real friendships, reputations, and momentum.

Influence Moves to Use:

  • Invest in Small, Genuine Moments: Text someone out of the blue to check in. Influence is built through unexpected warmth, not grand gestures.
  • Publicly Celebrate Others: Amplify friends' and teammates' wins without waiting for them to ask — recognition builds invisible loyalty.
  • Ask Real Questions, Listen Fully: Influence isn’t just about speaking — it’s about creating moments where people feel deeply heard.
  • Share Vulnerability First: To build deep bonds, be willing to share a little first — others follow vulnerability with vulnerability.
  • Lead Energy Without Owning the Spotlight: Be the person who brings energy to a group setting but doesn’t need credit — influence travels faster that way.

Mini Challenge:

Pick one person in your broader community you admire but aren't close with — and send them a quick, thoughtful compliment this week.

Questions to Ask Myself:

  • Am I helping others feel seen without expecting anything in return?
  • Have I amplified someone else's win this week?
  • Am I leading with humility, not spotlight-seeking?

carry with you the lessons you've learned.
